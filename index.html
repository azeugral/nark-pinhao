Nark Pinhão

Thirteen years of tattooing, with black and grey portraits as his signature: faces of people you love, icons, goddesses and original artwork.

He runs Versus Tattoo in Mafra and still paints walls with his crew. The best part of the job, in his words, is interpreting what a client imagines and turning it into personalized art.
